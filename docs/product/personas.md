# Personas

## The household investor

A Canadian investor downloads the French Wealthsimple activity CSV when they want to see contributions, fees, dividends, and what the accounts have been trading. They may hold registered accounts, a self-directed account, and cash. The app does not assume a particular set of account ids.

They are comfortable with a spreadsheet and impatient with one. They want the same answers a pivot table would give, and they want to click a year or a symbol instead of filtering by hand. They will notice if a deposit is counted twice or if a position pretends to be a live quote.

They read the interface in English. The export they download is French. Both have to work without them translating columns.

## The same person, six months later

They have a newer export. They do not want to rebuild a spreadsheet. They drop the file on the page, check that the date range moved forward, and expect the previous visit's file to still be there on that browser until they choose the synthetic sample again. The file never leaves the browser.
